# 🔐 Possessive Pronouns

> Los **Possessive Pronouns** expresan posesión y pueden reemplazar al noun para evitar repetirlo.

---

## 🎯 Objetivo

Al terminar este tema podrás:

* entender qué es un **Possessive Pronoun**;
* utilizar correctamente `mine`, `yours`, `his`, `hers`, `ours` y `theirs`;
* diferenciar **Possessive Adjectives** y **Possessive Pronouns**;
* entender por qué decimos `my computer`, pero `the computer is mine`;
* evitar errores como `mine computer`.

---

# 🤔 ¿Qué es un Possessive Pronoun?

Un **Possessive Pronoun** indica a quién pertenece algo.

Pero, a diferencia de un Possessive Adjective, **no necesita un noun después**.

Por ejemplo:

> This is **my computer**.

Podemos evitar repetir `computer`:

> This computer is **mine**.

Tenemos:

```text
my
↓
Possessive Adjective

mine
↓
Possessive Pronoun
```

---

# 🧩 Los Possessive Pronouns

| Subject Pronoun | Possessive Adjective | Possessive Pronoun |
| --------------- | -------------------- | ------------------ |
| I               | my                   | **mine**           |
| you             | your                 | **yours**          |
| he              | his                  | **his**            |
| she             | her                  | **hers**           |
| it              | its                  | —                  |
| we              | our                  | **ours**           |
| they            | their                | **theirs**         |

La relación principal es:

```text
I      → my      → mine
you    → your    → yours
he     → his     → his
she    → her     → hers
we     → our     → ours
they   → their   → theirs
```

---

# 🧠 La diferencia principal

## Possessive Adjective

Necesita un noun después.

```text
my computer
your book
her phone
our house
```

## Possessive Pronoun

Reemplaza al noun.

```text
mine
yours
hers
ours
```

Por eso:

> This is **my computer**.

pero:

> This computer is **mine**.

---

# 1️⃣ my → mine

### Possessive Adjective

> This is **my** computer.

Tenemos:

```text
my + computer
```

### Possessive Pronoun

> This computer is **mine**.

Aquí no repetimos `computer`.

```text
mine
↓
my computer
```

---

# 2️⃣ your → yours

### Possessive Adjective

> This is **your** book.

### Possessive Pronoun

> This book is **yours**.

Entonces:

```text
your + noun
↓
Possessive Adjective

yours
↓
Possessive Pronoun
```

---

# 3️⃣ his → his

Aquí aparece algo especial.

La forma es igual:

```text
his
```

para ambos casos.

### Possessive Adjective

> This is **his car**.

### Possessive Pronoun

> This car is **his**.

La palabra no cambia.

La función cambia.

---

# 4️⃣ her → hers

### Possessive Adjective

> This is **her** phone.

### Possessive Pronoun

> This phone is **hers**.

Tenemos:

```text
her + noun
↓
Possessive Adjective

hers
↓
Possessive Pronoun
```

---

# 5️⃣ our → ours

### Possessive Adjective

> This is **our** house.

### Possessive Pronoun

> This house is **ours**.

```text
our house
↓
Possessive Adjective + Noun

ours
↓
Possessive Pronoun
```

---

# 6️⃣ their → theirs

### Possessive Adjective

> This is **their** project.

### Possessive Pronoun

> This project is **theirs**.

Tenemos:

```text
their + project
```

pero:

```text
theirs
```

sin noun después.

---

# 🤔 ¿Y qué pasa con its?

Normalmente no utilizamos un Possessive Pronoun independiente equivalente a:

```text
its
```

Por eso nuestra tabla tiene:

```text
it → its → —
```

Por ejemplo, podemos decir:

> The dog eats **its food**.

Pero normalmente no usamos una estructura como:

> ❌ The food is its.

Por ahora simplemente recuerda:

> `its` funciona principalmente como **Possessive Adjective**.

---

# 🔑 Regla fundamental

Esta es probablemente la regla más importante del tema:

```text
Possessive Adjective
↓
needs a noun
```

```text
Possessive Pronoun
↓
replaces the noun
```

Ejemplo:

```text
my computer
↓
mine
```

```text
your book
↓
yours
```

```text
her car
↓
hers
```

---

# ⚠️ No digas "mine computer"

Este es uno de los errores más importantes que debemos evitar.

> ❌ mine computer

Lo correcto es:

> ✅ my computer

Porque antes de `computer` necesitamos:

> Possessive Adjective

Pero podemos decir:

> ✅ The computer is mine.

---

# 🔄 Evitar repetición

Los Possessive Pronouns son útiles porque evitan repetir información.

Por ejemplo:

> My computer is new. Your computer is old.

Podemos mejorar:

> My computer is new. **Yours** is old.

`yours` reemplaza:

> your computer

---

# 🧩 Otro ejemplo

> Her car is red. My car is blue.

Podemos decir:

> Her car is red. **Mine** is blue.

Aquí:

```text
mine
↓
my car
```

---

# 🧠 Possessive Adjective vs Possessive Pronoun

Esta tabla es muy importante:

| Possessive Adjective | Possessive Pronoun |
| -------------------- | ------------------ |
| my                   | mine               |
| your                 | yours              |
| his                  | his                |
| her                  | hers               |
| our                  | ours               |
| their                | theirs             |

Piensa:

```text
WITH NOUN
↓
my computer

WITHOUT NOUN
↓
mine
```

---

# 🔍 Cómo reconocerlos

Pregunta:

> **¿Hay un noun inmediatamente después?**

Si tenemos:

> my **computer**

Entonces:

> Possessive Adjective.

Si tenemos:

> This computer is **mine**.

No hay noun después de `mine`.

Entonces:

> Possessive Pronoun.

# 🔗 Conexión con lo aprendido

Ahora nuestra familia ya tiene cuatro columnas:

| Person         | Subject | Object | Possessive Adjective | Possessive Pronoun |
| -------------- | ------- | ------ | -------------------- | ------------------ |
| First Singular | I       | me     | my                   | mine               |
| Second         | you     | you    | your                 | yours              |
| Third Singular | he      | him    | his                  | his                |
| Third Singular | she     | her    | her                  | hers               |
| Third Singular | it      | it     | its                  | —                  |
| First Plural   | we      | us     | our                  | ours               |
| Third Plural   | they    | them   | their                | theirs             |

Por ejemplo:

```text
I → me → my → mine
```

Y:

```text
she → her → her → hers
```

# 🔎 Ejemplo completo con I

> **I** have a computer.

```text
I
↓
Subject Pronoun
```

> She called **me**.

```text
me
↓
Object Pronoun
```

> This is **my** computer.

```text
my
↓
Possessive Adjective
```

> This computer is **mine**.

```text
mine
↓
Possessive Pronoun
```

La misma persona.

Diferentes funciones.

---

# 🔎 Ejemplo completo con She

> **She** works here.

> I know **her**.

> This is **her** computer.

> This computer is **hers**.

Tenemos:

```text
she
↓
Subject

her
↓
Object

her
↓
Possessive Adjective

hers
↓
Possessive Pronoun
```

---

# ⚠️ His no cambia

Recuerda este caso:

```text
his car
```

y:

```text
the car is his
```

La palabra:

> `his`

puede funcionar como:

* Possessive Adjective;
* Possessive Pronoun.

El contexto nos indica cuál es.

---

# 🇪🇸 Comparación con español

En español podemos decir:

> Este es mi libro.

Y:

> Este libro es mío.

Tenemos algo parecido:

```text
mi
↓
my

mío
↓
mine
```

Por ejemplo:

> This is **my** book.

> This book is **mine**.

Esta comparación puede ayudarte al principio.

---

# ⚠️ No siempre traduzcas palabra por palabra

Aunque la comparación con español ayuda, lo más importante es reconocer el patrón:

```text
Possessive Adjective + Noun
```

versus:

```text
Possessive Pronoun
```

Así evitamos depender constantemente de traducir:

```text
my → mi
mine → mío
```

y empezamos a reconocer directamente su función.

---

# 🧠 Regla mental sencilla

Pregunta:

> **¿Voy a decir el noun después?**

### Sí

Utiliza:

```text
my
your
his
her
its
our
their
```

Ejemplo:

> my computer

### No

Puedes utilizar:

```text
mine
yours
his
hers
ours
theirs
```

Ejemplo:

> The computer is mine.

---

# 📝 Resumen

## Possessive Adjectives

Necesitan un noun:

```text
my computer
your book
his car
her phone
our house
their project
```

## Possessive Pronouns

Reemplazan al noun:

```text
mine
yours
his
hers
ours
theirs
```

La relación:

```text
my     → mine
your   → yours
his    → his
her    → hers
our    → ours
their  → theirs
```

---

# ✅ Debes recordar

1. Los **Possessive Pronouns** expresan posesión.
2. Son `mine`, `yours`, `his`, `hers`, `ours`, `theirs`.
3. No necesitan un noun después.
4. `my computer` es correcto.
5. `mine computer` es incorrecto.
6. `This computer is mine` es correcto.
7. `his` tiene la misma forma como Possessive Adjective y Possessive Pronoun.
8. Normalmente no utilizamos `its` como Possessive Pronoun independiente.
9. Los Possessive Pronouns ayudan a evitar repeticiones.
